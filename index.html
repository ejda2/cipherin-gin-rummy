How to Play

The Goal

Gin Rummy is played in a series of hands. Each hand you're racing to arrange your cards into melds (sets or runs) while minimizing the value of the cards left over. Whoever reaches 100 points first across all the hands wins the match.

The Deal

Each hand, you and the computer are dealt 10 cards. One card is turned face-up to start the discard pile; the rest form the stock pile, face-down. Players alternate who deals — and who goes first — from hand to hand.

Melds

  • A set — three or four cards of the same rank (e.g. 7♠ 7♥ 7♦)
  • A run — three or more consecutive cards of the same suit (e.g. 4♣ 5♣ 6♣)

Any card not part of a meld is deadwood, scored by rank: Ace = 1, 2–10 = face value, J/Q/K = 10.

Your Turn

Draw one card — from the stock (unseen) or the top of the discard pile — then discard one card from your hand. Play passes back and forth until someone ends the hand.

One restriction: if you drew from the discard pile, you can't immediately discard that exact same card back — hold it for at least this turn. Drawing from the stock has no such restriction.

Ending a Hand

  • Knock — if your discard leaves 10 or fewer deadwood points, you may knock and end the round.
  • Gin — if your discard leaves zero deadwood, you go out with Gin, a stronger result.
  • Wash — if the stock runs out with no knock, the hand ends with no score.

Scoring a Hand

If you knock: your opponent may lay off any deadwood cards that extend your melds first. Whoever has less deadwood after layoffs wins the hand and scores the difference. If your opponent's deadwood ends up equal to or lower than yours, they've undercut you — they win instead, plus a 25-point bonus.

If you go Gin: your opponent can't lay off anything. You score their full deadwood total, plus a 25-point bonus.

Winning the Match

Once someone's running score reaches 100, a scorecard totals the whole match:

Game Bonus100 points, awarded to whoever won the match
Line Bonus25 points for every hand you won
Shutout BonusIf your opponent never won a single hand, your final score doubles

Playing on This App

  • Draw: click the stock pile or the discard pile.
  • Discard: click a card in your hand to select it, then press the Space bar, tap the Discard button (for touch devices), or drag it onto the discard pile.
  • Knock: tap a card once to select it, then tap the Knock button.
  • Sort your hand: Sort by Suit or Sort by Value.
  • Melds are grouped and labeled automatically in Sort by Suit/Value.
  • If a discard would break up an already-formed meld, the app warns you first.
  • Players: create up to 10 computer opponents with their own name, playing style, and skill level. Click Stats next to any player to see your saved record against them.

A Few Tips

  • Low cards (Aces, 2s, 3s) are cheap to hold as deadwood while you wait for a meld to come together.
  • High cards hurt the most if you're stuck holding them — let go of ones that aren't working out.
  • Watch what your opponent picks up from the discard pile — it tells you what they need.
  • Knocking early is safer but caps your upside; going for Gin risks more but pays off bigger.
